About Captiviting views

Welcome to Discover Lesotho, your guide to exploring the beautiful Kingdom in the Sky! Our website introduces visitors to the natural beauty, cultural heritage and exciting tourist attractions of Lesotho.

Our purpose

Our mission is to promote tourism in Lesotho by providing useful information about tourist destinations, cultural experiences and exciting activities. We want to help visitors discover the beauty of our country and enjoy memorable travel experiences.

Why Visit Lesotho?

  • Beautiful Mountains: Explore the spectacular Maloti Mountains.
  • Tourist Attractions: Visit Maletsunyane Falls, Katse Dam and Sehlabathebe National Park.
  • Rich Culture: Experience Basotho traditions, music, clothing, food and local crafts.
  • Adventure Activities: Enjoy hiking, horseback riding and mountain biking.
  • Friendly People: Experience the warm hospitality of the Basotho people.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make Lesotho a popular tourist destination by showcasing its natural beauty, preserving its cultural heritage and supporting local communities through sustainable tourism.

Explore Lesotho with us! Whether you are looking for adventure, relaxation or cultural experiences, Discover Lesotho is here to help you explore the hidden treasures of the Kingdom in the Sky.

Frequently Asked Questions

Spring and autumn are great for hiking and sightseeing. From June to August, snow arrives in the highlands and Afriski opens for skiing and snowboarding. Summer (November to March) is best for waterfalls.

Visa rules depend on your passport. Many visitors can enter for short stays without a visa, but please check the requirements for your nationality before you travel and always carry your passport.

The local currency is the Loti (M), which is pegged to the South African rand. Rand is widely accepted, but carry some Loti for rural areas, where card machines can be scarce.

Main roads between towns are paved, but mountain roads can be rough and weather changes quickly. For places like Sani Pass, use a guide or a 4x4 and pack warm clothes.
